Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 May 2026 · Effective: 17 May 2026

The short version: We collect your name and email when you sign up. The ReWired app additionally collects screen time data, app usage, and approximate location to deliver its core features. We do not sell your data. You can request deletion at any time by emailing rewiredxapp@gmail.com.

3. How we store your data

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), operated by Google LLC. Firebase infrastructure used by ReWired is located within the European Economic Area (EEA). For more information on Google's data processing, see firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Waitlist form submissions are processed by Tally.so. Tally acts as a data processor on our behalf. For their privacy practices, see tally.so/privacy.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers, who process it on our behalf under data processing agreements:

  • Google Firebase — database, authentication, and backend infrastructure
  • Tally.so — waitlist and partner sign-up form processing

We may disclose data if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request from a competent authority.

5. Your rights under GDPR

As a data subject under EU law, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to restriction — ask us to pause processing of your data
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing

To exercise any of these rights, email us at rewiredxapp@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or analytics scripts. The Tally.so embedded forms may set functional cookies necessary for form operation. We do not use advertising or behavioural tracking cookies.

The ReWired app does not use cookies. App analytics, if introduced in future, will be disclosed in an updated version of this policy.

7. Children's privacy

ReWired is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at rewiredxapp@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify users by email where we hold an email address. Continued use of ReWired after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
